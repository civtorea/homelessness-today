Today, the Mental Wellness Center has expanded into a community mental health organization that combines education, housing, peer support, and direct services. In addition to operating the historic Fellowship Club, it provides residential housing across multiple sites for more than 100 community members, community wellness and recovery groups, family support services, employment support, youth mental health education, and public outreach programs designed to promote early intervention and reduce stigma. Its mission now extends beyond advocacy to include essential community services and safe, affordable housing as core components of mental wellness and long term recovery.
