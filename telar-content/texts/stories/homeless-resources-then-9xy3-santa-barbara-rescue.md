Today, the Santa Barbara Rescue Mission operates as a homelessness and addiction recovery organization rather than simply an emergency shelter. It provides year round homeless guest services, a state-licensed 12-month residential treatment program for men and women, Cornerstone Sober Living transitional housing, family support services, addiction education, relapse prevention, career development, and job placement. Recent expansions have also added daytime services, weekday case management for shelter guests, and transitional beds for individuals waiting to enter treatment, reflecting a broader approach to homelessness and recovery.
